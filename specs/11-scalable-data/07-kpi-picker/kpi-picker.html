The other layouts and the Scope buttons variants are earlier explorations, kept only for comparison. Drag the issue's bottom-right corner to try narrower widths.

Layouts

Two-pane browser
Looks like an ordinary select until you open it. Clicking or tabbing into the field expands a two-pane browser under it, like a big dropdown menu. The left menu lists only views tied to this work item: Tracked by parents, This space, then Parent spaces: the parents' spaces and, last, the central space (Global KPIs, if the site has one), the top of every chain. Then All KPIs. Other spaces (there may be hundreds) aren't listed: reach them through All KPIs. Typing searches the selected view, and says how many matches are elsewhere ("Search all KPIs"). All KPIs searches everything and has a Space filter you can type into. Picking a KPI, Esc or clicking outside collapses it. The scope-button choice doesn't apply.
Two-line list
The earlier option design: the KPI and its unit, plus a second line when there's a note.
Columns
One line per KPI: KPI · Unit, plus Target in "Tracked by parents".

Rules that hold in every variation

  1. Work items show as {type icon} {key} {summary}, like Jira Plans: KPIs and the parent work items. Icons arrive from the backend as inlined data: URLs (inlineIssueTypeIcons).
  2. "Tracked by parents" is grouped by work item. Each parent work item, nearest first, is a shaded band (not selectable). Every KPI it targets is a top-level row under the band; only their same-space sub-KPIs are indented. Targets never nest under each other, and each shows the target the work item set. A KPI targeted at two levels shows under both.
  3. Sub-KPIs start collapsed, everywhere: click a KPI's chevron (or press → / ←) to open or close it without picking it. Searching opens the path to each match.
  4. Nesting works as it does today: children are indented under their parent when both are listed. A KPI whose parent KPI isn't listed (e.g. it's in another space) is a top-level row, with no note.
  5. KPIs already tracked on the work item are left out.
  6. Paste a key or URL to jump to a KPI anywhere. Old keys of moved KPIs resolve (stage 03).

Data
