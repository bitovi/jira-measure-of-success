KPI picker across spaces · spec 11 stage 07 prototype

KPIs live in many company-managed spaces. The "Set a KPI target" picker suggests KPIs in your space first, then KPIs tracked above you (anything your parent chain targets), then other KPIs you can access. Each KPI appears once. Nesting is shown within a space. Use the controls to switch scenarios and try the open UX questions.

Rules shown here

  1. Each KPI appears once, in its highest section. A KPI in your space that's also tracked above keeps its place in section 1 and gets a Tracked by … badge.
  2. Nesting within a space works as it does today: children are indented under their parent.
  3. A parent in another space isn't pulled in. The child shows at the top of its own space with a hint, e.g. under PROG · Conversion rate.
  4. KPIs already tracked on the work item are left out.
  5. Search filters every section. "Other KPIs" results arrive after a short delay (a server search over every space you can browse).
  6. Paste a key or URL to jump straight to a KPI from anywhere. Old keys of moved KPIs resolve to the new key (stage 03).
