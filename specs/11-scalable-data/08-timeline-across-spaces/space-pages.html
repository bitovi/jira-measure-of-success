Spec 11·08 — KPI space pages (prototype)

Each Jira space gets a KPIs page (jira:projectPage). It lists this space's KPIs and loads their ancestors from other spaces, so a product sees how its KPIs ladder up through its portfolio to the global KPIs. Every KPI has one parent KPI. Nothing here talks to Jira. Click a space in the Jira sidebar, hover a row for +.

Questions for review (08 Q6–Q9)
  1. Parent-space KPIs: A (context rows), B (path bands) or C (A plus child spaces' KPIs beneath)?
  2. Portfolio pages: show the products' KPIs beneath the portfolio's KPIs (C) by default?
  3. Delivery teams: a full KPIs page, or "KPIs your work targets" when the team owns none (see Payments)?
  4. A space's parent space: derived from its KPIs' ancestors, or from its work items' parents (Initiative → Theme)?
  5. Context rows' values: show ancestors' current values and targets (costs their readings) or only names?
